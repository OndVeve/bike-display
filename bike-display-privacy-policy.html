Privacy Policy for Bike Display

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Bike Display ("we", "our", or "the App") is a cycling companion application that displays activity information received from compatible devices, including supported Garmin devices. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

The advertising and purchase sections below apply when those features are available in the version of the App you use.

1. Overview

The App does not require you to create an account. Cycling and connected-device information used for the core functionality is processed on your device and exchanged with compatible devices as needed. We do not operate a server that stores your cycling activity data.

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for app usage measurement and diagnostics. Versions that include advertising use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's consent tools. If you choose to buy the ad-free option, the purchase is processed through Google Play Billing.

2. Information Processed by the App

Depending on the features you use and your connected device, Bike Display may process:

  • speed and maximum speed;
  • distance and activity duration;
  • heart rate, cadence, and power;
  • elevation, ascent, and descent;
  • stamina or similar activity metrics provided by a compatible device;
  • navigation or route-related information provided by a compatible device;
  • battery and connection status; and
  • other cycling, fitness, sensor, or activity information transmitted by a connected device.

This information is used to provide the App's functionality and display cycling information to you. It is processed locally or exchanged directly between your phone and a connected device. We do not intentionally send cycling metrics, routes, or precise location to Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or AdMob.

3. Connected Devices and Bluetooth

The App may communicate with compatible devices using Bluetooth, Bluetooth Low Energy, Garmin Connect IQ, or other device communication mechanisms. Android may request nearby-device or other permissions required for discovery, connection, and communication. Information exchanged with a connected device is used to provide the functionality you request.

4. Location Permissions

Depending on your Android version, device, and communication technology, Android may require location-related or nearby-device permissions for Bluetooth discovery or connectivity. Such permissions are used for the relevant device functionality.

The App does not sell location information. The App does not intentionally transmit precise location or route information to us or to advertising services. Google services may use an IP address to estimate a device's general location, as described in Google's privacy information.

5. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and to improve its functionality. Analytics may process app interaction events, app and device information, and app-instance identifiers. Our manually logged events are limited to app interactions and technical states; they are not intended to include names, email addresses, GPS coordinates, routes, heart-rate readings, or other cycling metric values.

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify and diagnose crashes and stability problems. Crashlytics may process crash stack traces, relevant app state, app version, device model and operating-system information, and installation identifiers. Crash reports are sent to Google for processing and are made available to us in Firebase. Google states that Crashlytics crash data and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal begins; other Firebase data retention may depend on the service and its configuration.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy practices. See Firebase privacy and security and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising and Consent

In versions that include advertising, the App may display a banner advertisement in the Settings screen. Ads are not displayed on the active cycling dashboard. We do not intentionally provide cycling, fitness, health, route, or precise location information to AdMob for ad targeting.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process information such as the device's IP address, general location estimated from that address, ad or app identifiers, interactions with ads or the App, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. The information processed can depend on your device settings, the SDK configuration, your region, and your consent choices. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents choices about advertising and privacy. Depending on your region and choices, the App may request personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or limited ad serving. You can review or change available advertising privacy choices from the App's Settings screen when that option is provided.

Google's advertising services may process data under Google's own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

7. Remove Ads Purchase

Versions that offer the ad-free option allow you to make a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. We do not receive or store your payment card number or other full payment credentials.

The App receives purchase information, such as whether the purchase is active, so it can enable the ad-free entitlement and restore it when you reinstall the App or use another device with the same Google Play account. Google handles payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. See Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's payment information.

8. Information You Send Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond, provide support, and address App issues. We retain support correspondence only as reasonably necessary for those purposes, legal obligations, or the protection of our legitimate interests.

If you choose to use an optional external support or donation link, the payment or service provider handles information you provide to it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment credentials through that link.

9. Other Third-Party Services

The App may rely on services provided by Google and Android, Garmin and Garmin Connect IQ, and your device manufacturer or operating-system provider. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Bike Display is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garmin Ltd. Garmin and related trademarks are the property of their respective owners.

10. Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information or cycling/activity data. Advertising SDK providers may receive or process information as described in the Advertising and Consent section for their advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation purposes.

11. Data Retention

We do not retain cycling or connected-device activity information on our servers. Information processed by Firebase and AdMob is subject to Google's service-specific retention practices and settings. Support correspondence may be retained as described above. Purchase and transaction records are handled by Google Play; the App stores or checks only the information needed to determine whether the ad-free entitlement is active.

12. Data Security

Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). We take reasonable measures in designing and operating the App, but no software or electronic communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Children's Privacy

Bike Display is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the core functionality of the App. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

14. Your Choices and Rights

You can manage permissions granted to Bike Display in your Android device settings. Where available, you can change advertising privacy choices from the App's Settings screen. You can also control or reset the Android advertising ID through Android settings.

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of, restriction of, or objection to processing of personal information we hold. Contact us using the details below. Since most cycling information is processed locally and is not held by us, we may not have activity data to retrieve or delete.

You can manage or cancel Google Play purchases and request purchase support through Google Play. Uninstalling the App does not cancel or refund a purchase.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its features, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at the Privacy Policy URL associated with the App. The date at the top indicates when it was most recently revised.
